# Missing keywords: decisions (after the 14.10 effect box audit)

Proposed one at a time; built together once every one is decided.

1. **Mechanical Units** (21 Oct): option 1 approved. Add **Troop** and **Hero** as narrowing words
   (*target Troop ally*, *all Hero allies within 1*). On your side "Mechanical Units" reads as your
   Troops (Revision 15.5: every Troop is a mechanical robot), so Revision 15.5 stands. Cards that
   check an Enemy's make-up (Bonk!, Bigger Bonk!, Rend Asunder) stay flagged.
   Boxes it covers: Burning Heart (Hero part), CERBERUS Overlord Bot's Battle presence, Dragonskin
   lining, Emergency Overdrive (narrowing only), Frenzy Protocol (with "all allies"), Overclock Surge,
   Redline overdrive (with "all allies"), Reroute Power (with "all allies"), Scavenge Parts,
   SecurMax's Shoulder to Shoulder (narrowing only), Scrap Caddy, Repair Kit, Reinforced Frames
   (with "all allies").

2. **SPOOL** (21 Oct): option 2 approved. No new keyword; SPOOL is dropped and its cards are rewritten
   with the Stats keywords already in the library (stacking, the cap of 3 and the two Governors are
   lost). Rictus's Spin-Up, Grinder, Counterweight Fist, Kickstart, Overrun, Redline Governor and its
   Purge get rewrites, shown for approval with the rest; Spool Governor and Overdrive Governor have
   nothing left to do and need new effects (or retiring).

3. **Gaining Aether** (21 Oct): option 1 approved. One new Effect, **gain Aether X**: Aether of the
   card's own District (Neutral if it has none). Covers Aether Battery, Debt Ledger, Ledger Core,
   Ledger Chip, and the two Salvage cards (as gain Aether 1). Alchemical Chip and Split Chip stay
   flagged.

4. **Everyone on one side** (21 Oct): option 1 approved. New Targets **all allies** and **all enemies**,
   with no distance: the whole Battlefield; they take narrowing words; scored like an area of 4 (x3).
   Covers Frenzy Protocol, Hold the Line, Redline overdrive, Reroute Power, Field Triage, Reinforced
   Frames; Call the Note in part (its bonus only against marked targets stays flagged).

5. **To-Hit** (21 Oct): option 1 approved. A new Stat, **Aim** (signed): +2 Aim means the Unit's ranged
   attacks need 2 less to hit (the mirror of Evasive). Covers Deploy bipod, Steady aim, Grid
   Discipline, Targeting Mesh. Whisper Coil (range 4+) and Signal Flare (against one Enemy) stay flagged.

6. **Drawing cards** (21 Oct): option 1 approved. A new Effect, **Draw X**: draw X cards from your Deck
   into your hand; nothing on an Enemy's card. Covers Datamine, Ledger Call, Ledger Runner, Scavenger
   Protocol. Sense Steel and Liquidate stay flagged.

7. **Taking less damage** (21 Oct): option 1 approved, named **Hardened** (not Plated). A new Status,
   **Hardened X**: each hit on the Unit deals X less, never below 1; lasts its Duration, and holds from
   an always-on Passive. Covers Dampening Chip (target ally gains Hardened 1) and Dross's Slag Armor
   (this Unit gains Hardened 1). Harmonic Damper (Aether only) stays flagged.

8. **Melee-only damage** (21 Oct): option 1 approved. No new keyword: MONTU Battle Suit, Superheated
   Blades (and Counterweight Fist's rewrite) keep DMG, which raises every attack the Unit makes.

9. **Placing Cover** (21 Oct): option 1 approved. A new Effect, **Barricade** (Half or Full): puts that
   Cover Barricade on the target tile, which must be empty. Covers Deploy FCB and Deploy HCB. BRICK Wall
   stays flagged.

10. **Lines and cones** (21 Oct): option 1 approved. New Modifiers **Line X** (every Unit on the X tiles
    straight from the attacker through the target) and **Cone X** (every Unit in the widening wedge X
    tiles deep toward the target); both hit friend and foe, as AoE does. Covers Aether Lance and Slag spray.

11. **The one-offs** (21 Oct): option 1 approved. Each card with a gap of its own is rewritten with the
    keywords we have plus the ten decided above, keeping the card's idea; every rewrite goes on the review
    page for approval. Cards with no sensible rewrite stay flagged.
