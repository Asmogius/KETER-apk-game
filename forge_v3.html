These three are the card's Aether cost. Any Affinity is paid with Aether from any District; Dedicated Affinity must be paid from this card's own District. Both print as pips in the top-right corner.

A second Affinity sweeps the card colour diagonally, first Affinity at the top-left. Levels are capped at 6 across both.

▶Stat Bar

In Handbook v11 printed order. Placeholders show the Handbook's typical range. Values run wider than 3 characters at your own risk — the cell shrinks the digits to fit.

▶Effects

Damage is its own field, not prose, so the scaler can find it. Action Cards carry one Active and one Passive; units carry up to four. Everything else comes from equipped Action Cards. Fewer entries means a taller art window.
