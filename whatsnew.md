## v14.23 — Clear the Districts

Alongside game 0.38.

- **Each District has a Core** (★), its tile farthest from the dock: a POI of three floors, the last a Boss's where one fits.
- **Beat it to clear the District for good**: 5 merits for the Colony Rank, and the Core never comes back.
- **Dimensional Instability arrives**: 3% in a deployment's fights for every District cleared, 15% at most.
- The World Map says how many of the six are cleared. Clear them all and the colony is safe.

## v14.22 — Colony Rank, Mk II and Mk III

Alongside game 0.37.

- **Colony Rank**: coming home earns merits (1, and 1 more for every second Skull Level reached); 3, 7, 12, 18 and 25 merits make Ranks 2 to 6. On a deployment a team fights at the rank's Player Level; a Loadout's own Player Level is for practice. The Dock shows the rank under the stockpile.
- **Mk II and Mk III**: the Workshop raises a design a Mark with Troop parts (+3 Health and +1 Armor Class; then +6, +2 and +1 Movement in all). The starter too. A copy starts at Mk I.
- The Workshop marks the designs that go out free (costing only Common parts).

## v14.21 — Four of a card, shorter early fights

Alongside game 0.36.

- **4 copies of a card at most** in a Loadout's Deck, Troops included, and of a Chip that is a card in the Pouch (Basic Chips aren't limited). A Loadout from before is trimmed.
- **The Starter bay** now frees the copies of your dearest designs (1, 2 or 3 of them) on every deployment, instead of more Frame Troopers.
- **A deployment's fight has fewer waves where it's easy**: 3, 3, 4, 4, 5, 6 by Skull Level, one fewer in an ambush.
- **A gentler start**: at Skull 1 and 2 two Heroes may enter any floor, and the Enemy spawn tiles take turns, wave by wave.

## v14.20 — More loot, free Common cards, a first team step by step

Alongside game 0.35.

- **More loot.** Every Enemy, Elite, Mini-Boss, Boss, Loot Tile and Locked Chest drops about twice the shards and parts; a Boss's first two parts come at the highest rarity it can reach. Caches and POIs give twice as much, and drop a Consumable or Prototype more often, as do Elites, Mini-Bosses and Chests. The Bounty Board pays twice as much.
- **Rarer parts sooner**: from Skull 2 up, more of each drop is Uncommon or rarer.
- **Cheaper at the dock**: parts, blueprints, chassis and recruiting cost about 40% less; crafting costs fewer shards and parts.
- **Common cards are free.** A deployment takes Common Equipment, Command, Aether and Action cards and Chips without the stash; a Common Troop card goes without the Workshop; and a design whose copies cost only Common parts costs nothing to take. The starter stash is Uncommon cards now.
- **A first Strike Team, one step at a time.** With no Loadout yet, the Deck Builder walks a new one through: name, Heroes, Deck (Suggest a Deck fills it with Common cards), Action Cards, Pouch (Suggest a Pouch), and a last look.

## v14.19 — The stash

Alongside game 0.34.

- **A deployment's cards come from the stash.** The Equipment, Command and Aether cards in its Deck, the Action cards its Heroes carry and the Chips in its Pouch must be held at the dock, or are crafted as it leaves. They go out with the team, come home with it, and are lost if it is wiped out. Troops, Consumables and Prototypes work as before.
- **Craft, a new tab at the Dock**: any of those cards, for shards and parts of its rarity (Equipment parts for Equipment, Aether parts for Aether cards and Chips; Command and Action cards cost twice the shards instead). The tab lists what the stash holds. Deploy shows what the team takes from the stash and what is crafted for it.
- **A new run begins with a starter stash**, and the pick of one of three rare cards. A run with deployments behind it is given what its Strike Teams need instead.
- **Dev Mode** sets the crafting prices, the starter stash and how many rare cards are offered.

## v14.8 — Movement keywords

Handbook Revision 16.1 adds four keywords for Units that fight on the move, and the Battle Simulator and game 0.21 carry them out.

- **Lunge X** (on a Melee Action): the attack can reach a target X tiles further off, the Unit walking up to X tiles to stand beside it first, free.
- **Charge X** (on a Melee Action): X more damage once the Unit has moved 3 tiles or more this turn. A Lunge's tiles count.
- **Dash X**: X more tiles of Movement this turn, before or after acting. An Enemy Dashes away at once from the Unit it struck.
- **Leap X**: jumps up to X tiles over Units, Cover, walls and gaps. Your Units' lit tiles include the jumps.
- **In the Card Forge and the keyword builder** they read and score like any other keyword; Lunge and Charge are offered only on a Melee Action.
- **Two new test cards** use them: the **Test Skirmisher** (a Troop) and the **Test Skitter** (a Skull 2 Enemy that lunges, rakes, and skitters away).

Game 0.21 arrives alongside, carrying them out exactly as the Simulator does.

## v14.0 — The playtest's fixes

A playtest of the action creator found ways to build abilities that won every fight or cost nothing. Handbook Revision 16.0 closes them.

- **Locks: Stunned, Rooted, Pinned and Marked.** Each lasts 2 turns at most. A second dose does nothing while the first lasts. Once it ends, the Unit can't be given the same lock again until the same point of the next round, and the Simulator shows a hollow ring beside its Status dots while that guard lasts. An always-on Passive can't give a lock. The keyword builder offers only 1 or 2 turns for them, and the Card Forge flags anything longer.
- **What an attack carries needs it to Penetrate.** The Statuses, Pushes and extra damage written on a Melee, Ranged or Cyber attack land only when it gets through Armor Class (Integrity, for Cyber). Support Actions always land.
- **Execute**, a new Effect: it removes an Enemy below half its Health outright, never an Elite, a Mini-Boss or a Boss. The builder offers it only for Enemy Targets, and it costs about what heavy damage does.
- **Sacrifice removes only your own Units**, keeps the other effects in its sentence, and no longer makes an ability cheaper.
- **Fairer prices in the Workshop and the Forge.** An area effect costs ×1.5, ×2 or ×2.5 for a reach of 1, 2 or 3 tiles. A surer shot costs more: to-hit now multiplies an attack's power by its chance to hit, instead of a harder shot costing more.
- **A chassis sets a best (lowest) to-hit.** Its designs' attacks choose from that up to 20. Chassis made before keep their ranking: an old maximum of 8 reads as a best of 4.
- **Overclock is out of the builder.** It has no rules in a fight yet. Where a card already has it, the Forge says it does nothing, and the rest of the effect still works.
- **The master Enemies aim better.** Their Ranged to-hits were 14 to 18, so they hit about one shot in four; they are 8 to 10 now.

Game 0.13 arrives alongside, with the same fixes. A guarded Unit wears its ring on the board, and the side panel says what it is guarded against.

## v13.6 — A deployment loses only the copies it took

- **A Troop that falls costs a copy only of a design the team took, and only while one is left.** When a Troop fell in a deployment's fight, the dock counted a copy lost of any design with its name, taken or not: a Troop that another card's Deploy put down cost a copy of a design the team never took. And once the last copy was gone, each Troop of that name that fell after it was counted again, with its own *A copy of … is lost* in the deployment's log, so a design could lose more copies than went out. ## v13.5 — When a Status ticks, and Auto plays whole rounds

- **A Status ticks at the end of the turn after the one it arrived in** (Handbook 15.9). Everything used to tick at the Recharge, so a Status an Enemy gave you lost a turn before you had taken one: a Hero Stunned for a turn was never Stunned in a turn of their own. Now a Status that arrives in the Enemies' turn ticks as you end your next turn, and the Log shows it there. One that arrives in your turn ticks at the Recharge, as before. Timed Stats changes follow the same rule.
- **Auto plays whole rounds.** Auto went from the Enemies straight back to your Units and skipped everything between. No wave after the first ever arrived, so a fight with more waves to come ran sixty empty rounds and ended as a Draw. No Aether was generated after the opening, no cards or Chips were drawn, and no Status ever ticked, so a Unit Stunned for a turn stayed Stunned for the whole fight. Auto now runs every phase the played game does.
- **The round ends once.** With no Enemy left to act in the Enemy turn (a cleared wave with more to come), the round was ended twice: a hazard bit whoever stood on it twice, and a Hero's Energy, which carries over, was refilled twice. It is ended once.
- **Two Units of one name holding fire are two lines in the Log.** The second line was dropped as a repeat.
- **A POI's final reward no longer repeats its last floor.** The reward's blueprint or chassis was chosen without counting what the last floor had just given, so it could be the same one again, and the POI's reward was wasted. The game found this one too, walking the same deployments as the Companion and comparing.

Game 0.8 arrives alongside: the World Map and a deployment.


## v13.4 — Variable costs work, and Usability Restrictions are gone

- **Usability Restrictions are removed** (Handbook 15.8). Any Hero may carry any Action card, any Unit may wear any Equipment, any Troop may perform any Command. The Forge's **Usable by** box is gone, Equipment, Action and Command cards no longer take Archetypes, and their type line reads *Any Hero*, *Any Unit* or *Any Troop*.
- **Variable costs are played.** A card that costs X asks for X as you play it, from 1 up to what the Bank can pay. Its text is carried out with that number wherever a keyword says X: *damage X*, *+X HP*, *for X turns*, *Deploy X Aether Battery*.
- **A stat can be N(X).** A Troop with Health 5(X) played for X = 3 arrives with 15. One that arrives unpaid, as a Starting Troop or from a Deploy, has X = 1.
- **The Forge offers X.** On a card with a variable cost the keyword builder's number step has **Use X**. It flags X on a card with no variable cost, and warns when a card costs X but only says so in plain words.
- **A variable cost is saved properly.** It used to be saved as a 0 and a flag, which lost which slot the X stood in. Cards saved that way open with the X in the first empty slot, Any Affinity first: check yours.
- **Dimensional Instability sends what the District has.** The Elite or Mini-Boss comes from the floor's own District. With none of the kind rolled, the ordinary Enemy arrives and the Log says so. It used to borrow from any District.
- **The Pouch check counts X as 1.** A variable-cost card used to count as free.
- **Auto and carried Action cards.** On Auto, a Hero pays the Aether a carried Action card costs, or doesn't use it, and a Burst makes all of its attacks. It used to make one, and pay nothing.
- **Stunned stops an attack too.** A Stunned Enemy couldn't move, but on its own turn it still attacked whoever it could reach from where it stood. A Stunned Unit of yours did the same on Auto. The Handbook says Stunned "cannot act or move at all", so now either one loses its turn and the Log says so; an Enemy's Combo, interrupted, starts again. A Unit Stunned after taking aim doesn't take its held shot. (Your own Units' buttons were already greyed.)
- **Auto finds loot.** One of your Units that Auto leaves standing on a Loot Tile or a Locked Chest finds what is there. Before, only a Unit you moved by hand did. The game found this one: it plays the same fights and compares what dropped.

Game 0.7 arrives alongside: Dimensional Instability, loot shown as Enemies fall, Auto for your Units, and variable costs.


## v13.3 — Recover, and the Simulator plays by the Handbook in more places

- **Recover**, a new Effect keyword (Handbook 15.7). It returns a Troop's card from the Discard Pile to your hand, to be played again at its normal cost. It is what the Handbook's Repair Kit does, and until now no card could be written to do it. The Forge offers it with the other Effects. A card that Recovers asks which Troop when there is more than one, and can't be played while the Discard Pile holds none.
- **A Troop's card goes to the Discard Pile when the Troop goes down**, not when it's played. Equipment goes there when it's removed or its Unit goes down. Starting Troops count too, so one that falls can be Recovered.
- **Consumables do what they say.** Tap one to read it, then **Use it**: its keywords are carried out, with a target where it needs one. Before, it was only marked used and its text logged. A Consumable written in plain text is still logged as not carried out.
- **A carried Action card costs its Aether** as well as its Energy, at every use, as Section 3 says. Its button shows both, and greys while the Bank can't pay.
- **Chips and Consumables are used on your turn**, like cards. A Chip is spent when its effect happens, not when it's tapped, so backing out of choosing its target no longer wastes it.
- **A card whose Deploy names nothing is named in the problem box.** Played from the hand, it used to name the Hero leading ("Deploy on Test Warden does not name what to deploy"), which sent you to the wrong card.
- **Paying with Generic Aether no longer jams the Bank.** Playing a card of a District you held no Aether of, paid for with Generic, left the Bank unable to pay for anything else that turn, a free card included. This was in the rules themselves (`rules.js`), and is fixed there.

Game 0.6 arrives alongside: cards played from the hand, Consumables, Chip effects, and Undo for all three.


## v13.2 — Two Enemy-turn fixes in the Battle Simulator

Both were found by the KETER game, which runs the same fights through its own
copy of these rules and compares the two.

- **An Enemy still out of reach after its move no longer attacks.** It used to
  strike with its next Action from wherever it stood, so a melee Enemy could
  land a blow from ten tiles away. Now it moves, and the log says it has
  nothing in reach.
- **An Enemy brought down as it moves no longer attacks.** A held shot
  (Suppressive Fire) that took an Enemy to 0 Health mid-move didn't stop it
  making its attack. Now it's down, and that's the end of its turn.

Nothing else changes. Game 0.5 arrives alongside: Strike Teams built and saved
in the game, six waves, Aether and the draw each round, and a hand you can read.

## v12.3 — Conditions as grammar, always-on Passives (Handbook 15.6)

- **Conditions are grammar now**: a connector — **when** (fires each time an
  event happens), **if** (checks as the effect resolves) or **while** (on for as
  long as a state holds) — then who it is about, then an event or a state:
  *When this Unit takes damage, …*, *If the target has Slowed, …*. New events:
  deals damage, attacks, is attacked, moves, ends its move within N tiles,
  gains a Status, the round begins; new states: below half HP, at full HP, has
  a Status, within / further than N tiles. One *when* and one *if* at most.
- **Passives are always on** while their card is active — a Unit standing,
  Equipment attached, a Prototype in the fight. Their Stats need no Duration;
  damage or healing happens once a round, at Recharge. A *when* Passive reacts
  each time, and never sets itself off. **Passives are exempt from subtypes.**
- **Narrowing words**: subtypes, every Archetype (custom ones too) and
  *District N* narrow a Target — *target Sniper ally*, *all District 3 allies
  within 2 tiles*. The Simulator only lets you aim at Units that match.
- **The keyword builder**: *+ Build an effect* in the Forge walks you through
  one effect at a time, offering only what is legal, with the power live and a
  review at the end. Tap an effect to reopen it. The Chassis editor's *Build*
  offers only what the chassis allows.
- **Cards read better**: distances say *tiles*, durations *for 2 turns*, a
  sentence opens with a capital, and a keyword's number never wraps away from it.
- **Enemies can have Passives and Auras** in the Forge; they were being read
  as Combos.
- **Test cards** use the new grammar and exercise it: always-on Passives, a
  *while*, *when … if*, *the round begins*, *ends its move within*, *gains
  Slowed*, *has Slowed*, a narrowed Aura-style Prototype, and a new **Test Rally**
  (all Ranged allies within 3 tiles). Their checklists say what to expect.
- Older cards' one-piece Conditions (*if damaged*, *when deployed*…) are
  rewritten as grammar when they load, and chassis allow lists grow to match.

**Your phone now gets the current card list.** The app only takes a new card

**Handbook 15.4**

- **Seven keyword categories.** New are **Modifier**, in green — Burst, AoE,

## v7.6

  Datapad, the Card Forge and the Handbook, which is how the Energy rule ended
  up wrong in one of them. There is now a single resolver that the Datapad
  calls and a simulator will call, so the two cannot disagree.
- Nothing changes on screen. The Datapad behaves exactly as it did.

## v7.5

- **Enemy Behavior.** Enemy cards now carry keywords that decide how they are
  run: Cunning, Sadistic, Opportunistic, Brutish, Vindictive, Ravenous,
  Methodical, Cautious X and Cowardly X. They stack, and a conditional keyword
  outranks one that always applies.
- Keywords print on the card; the Forge flags combinations that cannot work.
- Fixed: **Boss cards were labelled MINI-BOSS** and shared their code letter.

## v7.4

- **A Combat Datapad Guide**, which the app had been shipping without.
- **Fixed an Energy rule I had got wrong.** The Handbook says Troops reset their
  Energy each turn while Heroes carry over what they do not spend. The Datapad
  was carrying over for everything.
- The Handbook now settles **healing against gaining Health**: healing stops at
  the printed figure, a gain goes above it.
- Guide version labels are derived from the build rather than typed, so they
  stop drifting.

## v7.3

- **Actions pick a target and resolve themselves.** Damage, healing and Health
  gains are read off the card and applied to whoever you choose — the other
  side is offered first for an attack, your own for a heal.
- **Statuses change the stats they should.** Overwhelmed halves Damage and
  Movement Speed, and an Overwhelmed attacker's Action lands at half
  automatically. Compromised shows Security at 0 with Passives off.
- **Healing and gaining Health are different things.** Healing stops at the
  starting figure; a Health gain goes past it, and the bar turns green.
- Fixed: **an imported symbol could be made but never chosen** in the POI
  Sandbox. Selecting one threw, because a stencil is not a drawn pictogram and
  two places assumed it was.

## v7.2

- **Fixed "Add my cards to Master" — the button that was actually stripping
  them.** It was the last path still handing over the list summary rather than
  the card. Adding cards now waits for the Card Forge, which is the only thing
  that knows how to write one, so the whole card comes across whether or not
  you have opened the Forge tab.
- **Checkboxes are visible again.** The same iOS fix that flattened them in the
  Card Forge was doing it here too, which is why the review list looked as
  though it had nothing to tick.

## v7.1

- **Fixed the stripped-card problem at its root.** The routine that clears out
  cards added by older versions was only running when you changed your username,
  so old damaged entries stayed in the Master forever and every publish sent
  them out again. It now runs when the app opens.
- Open the app once, let it clear the Master, then add your cards again from the
  Compendium — they will come across whole.
- The repair notice is a banner on the Home screen rather than a dialog thrown
  over whatever you were reaching for.

## v7.0

- **Actions confirm before they fire.** Tapping one shows what it does, its cost
  and its damage, with Use it or Cancel.
- **Enemy Combos are tracked.** The strip shows which Action is next, with
  Advance and Restart — an interrupted Combo restarts at step one, which is the
  rule a table always forgets. A Special Ability overrides the next Action
  without advancing the sequence.
- **Statuses come from a list** with their definitions, a duration, and a
  warning when one cannot apply — a Cyborg cannot be Overwhelmed.
- Fixed: **Energy was resetting each turn.** +NRG is what a Unit generates, and
  unspent Energy carries over.
- **Publishing the Master is now guarded.** A Master holding cards with no rules
  text is what produced a stripped compendium; it is caught before it goes out.

## v6.9

- **The Combat Datapad reads your cards.** Add a unit and pick one from your
  collection: Health, Energy, Will or Security and its subtype all come with it.
- **Its Actions come too**, as buttons that spend the Energy they cost. An
  Action you cannot pay for is greyed out and says how short you are.
- It now knows what a status means — a Cyborg carries Security and cannot be
  Overwhelmed, and *Compromised* spells out what it switches off.

## v6.8

- **The unicode symbols are gone.** They could not be printed and did not
  survive being shared. Fifty **drawn** pictograms replace them, in five groups.
- The pictogram library **scrolls** properly — the top and bottom are no longer
  cut off.
- The POI Sandbox **floor readout is draggable**: resize it, or tap to fold it
  away. The tool row scrolls sideways instead of wrapping onto three lines.
- Together those give the map **80% of the screen instead of 61%**, or 87% with
  the readout collapsed.

## v6.7

- **Combat Datapad.** Track a whole fight: both sides, HP, Energy, statuses and
  the round. Next turn refreshes Energy, counts down statuses and clears the
  damage counters — the three things a table always forgets.
- It counts **instances** of damage, not points, so Overwhelmed triggers when it
  should. Mechanical and Cyborg units are exempt.
- Everything is logged. **Export the log** and a run of fights will tell you how
  long they last and what actually kills things.

## v6.6

- **World Map Tile cards.** The hexagon you cut out is drawn into the art
  window, coloured by Affinity, with a name band and a symbol.
- **One symbol library across both tools.** The pictograms, and anything you
  import in the Sandbox, are now available on tile cards as well.

## v6.5

- **Import your own symbols** in the POI Sandbox. Any image is reduced to a
  monochrome stencil with a threshold slider and a live preview, so it prints
  like the drawn pictograms and is small enough to travel with a shared floor.

## v6.4

- **Guides and rules brought up to date.** The Handbook now documents
  Archetypes as targeting labels, combat keywords and variable X costs, and no
  longer calls Mechanical an Archetype or lists the retired Mechanized Unit.
- The Card Forge guide covers subtypes, Security, keywords, Auras, editing on
  the card, Equipment modifiers and the Card Files sorting.
- The Sandbox guide covers shapes, mirror, multi-mirror and the zoom control.
- Fixed: **an Aether Chip looked identical at every Level**, because the face
  failed to draw and the failure was being swallowed silently.
- Removed a ping that told you to add a "Mechanical" Archetype that no longer
  exists.

## v6.3

- The app is **120 KB smaller and loads a third faster**. The two tools were
  still stored as base64, which costs a flat 33% for nothing.
- Fixed: **Help labelled only 5 of the Sandbox's 30 visible controls.** The
  dimming layer behind the open panel was answering for the controls Help had
  just revealed, and each label drawn hid the next one behind it.
- Restored several fixes that had been silently lost: the pinned Help button,
  the removal of duplicate Help, Ping, Task and Pan buttons, and the fix that
  made checkboxes render at all.

## v6.2

- **Multi-mirror.** With mirror on, the ⬡ button cycles the symmetry: one
  line, a cross for four-way, or six slices. Every plane is drawn, so what you
  see is what you get.

## v6.1

- **Report a bug**, on the Home screen. The app keeps a rolling record of the
  last 200 things you pressed and anything that went wrong, then hands you a
  report to copy. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Fixed: an **Aura printed as ACTION** on the card.
- Fixed: the app was reporting itself as v4.8 regardless of the actual version,
  which also broke the update check.
