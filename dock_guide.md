# The Dock

The Dock is home base between deployments. It's where you trade at the
**Exchange**, turn parts into other parts by **Reclaiming**, take jobs from
the **Bounty Board**, and improve the dock itself with **Upgrades**. It's part
of the video game, not the board game, and none of it is in the Handbook.

Open it from **Dock** on Home, or from the **Dock** button on the World Map
while your Strike Team is at home.

## Closed while you're out

The dock is closed while a Strike Team is out. You can open the page and
look, but buying, selling, reclaiming, upgrades and Workshop research all wait
until the team comes home.

## Your reach

Your **reach** is the highest Skull Level a haul has come home from. It
starts at none. It only rises when a Strike Team gets home; a wipe doesn't
count.

Reach sets the rarest component the dock deals in:

| Reach | Rarest component |
| --- | --- |
| None, or Skull 1 | Common |
| Skull 2 | Uncommon |
| Skull 3 | Rare |
| Skull 4 | Epic |
| Skull 5 | Legendary |
| Skull 6 | Mythic |

## The Exchange

The Exchange sells six kinds of things. Everything is paid in Aether shards.

### Components

- **Commons** of every part type are always in stock, in any amount.
- **Lots of rarer parts**: a few limited lots, each of one part type and one
  rarity, never rarer than your reach. A lot sells out.

| Rarity | Price each |
| --- | --- |
| Common | 6 |
| Uncommon | 15 |
| Rare | 40 |
| Epic | 100 |
| Legendary | 250 |
| Mythic | 600 |

### Blueprints and chassis

A couple of blueprints and a chassis, drawn from the pools of the Districts
you've fought in. Only what you could use is offered: the next blueprint level
you're missing, or a chassis you don't have.

**Seeing one on the shelf names it.** A level 1 blueprint you've never found
has no name until then. Once it's named, the Codex and the Workshop show it,
even if you don't buy it.

| Item | Price |
| --- | --- |
| A blueprint you haven't found (level 1) | 150 |
| A later level | its research price × 1.25: 75 for level 2, 150 for level 3 |
| A chassis | 300 |

Buying one grants it at once. It's yours in the Workshop.

### Consumables and Prototypes

Consumable and Prototype cards, a few at a time, from a list set in Dev Mode.
Buy as many as you like. What you buy is **yours**, kept at the dock:

- **A Consumable** is used up once it's used in a fight.
- **A Prototype** isn't used up. It comes home with the team, unless the team
  is wiped out.

You choose which of them to take on each deployment. See **Packing**, below.

### Pre-built Troops

Finished Troop designs made by the developer, so you can try combinations you
can't build yet: keywords you haven't found, chassis you don't own.

- **Buy as many copies as you can afford.** The Exchange never runs out.
- **Copies wait at the dock**, then join your **next** deployment, on top of
  its Deck. They're gone when that deployment ends, home or wiped.
- **A copy lost in a fight** is gone for the rest of the deployment, like any
  other copy.
- **A copy costs 1.5 ×** what its parts would cost at the Exchange's prices.

Three are offered at a time, in the order they were made. Each restock moves
on to the next three.

### Selling

The Exchange buys back **components**, **Consumables** and **Prototypes** at
**half** its price. It doesn't buy blueprints or chassis.

### When it restocks

The Exchange restocks after a deployment that **revealed new ground**: at
least one tile you hadn't seen before. That's true whether the team came home
or was wiped out. A deployment that only walked known ground doesn't restock
it.

A restock rolls new lots, new blueprints and chassis, moves the Consumables,
Prototypes and pre-builts on to the next few in their lists, and posts a new
Bounty Board.

## Reclaiming

Turn parts you have into parts you need:

- **Combine**: 3 of one rarity make 1 of the next rarity, same part type.
  Never rarer than your reach.
- **Swap**: 2 of one part type make 1 of another part type, same rarity.

## The Bounty Board

Three bounties at a time: two **Enemies** and one **Elite, Mini-Boss or
Boss**. Each is an Enemy you've met, from a District you've fought in.

- **Beat it in a won fight on a deployment**, or before an Emergency Exit.
- **Get home** and it pays. A wipe loses the claim.
- **It pays shards and components**, both scaled by the Skull Level it fell
  at: shards per Skull Level, and components rolled at that level.

| Kind | Shards per Skull Level | Components |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enemy | 15 | 1 |
| Elite | 30 | 2 |
| Mini-Boss | 60 | 3 |
| Boss | 120 | 5 |

A bounty pays once. The board changes when the Exchange restocks.

## Upgrades

Each upgrade has three levels, paid in shards and parts:

| Upgrade | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Exchange stock** | +1 rare lot and pre-built on offer | +2 | +3 |
| **Starter bay** | 6 free starter copies | 8 | 10 |
| **Salvage insurance** | keep 10% of a wiped haul | 20% | 30% |
| **Long-range scanner** | see 2 steps away | 3 | 4 |

| Level | Costs |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 150 shards and 10 Common parts |
| 2 | 400 shards and 6 Uncommon parts |
| 3 | 900 shards and 3 Rare parts |

The parts are Equipment parts for the Exchange and the scanner, Troop parts
for the Starter bay, and Aether parts for insurance.

- **Starter bay** raises how many copies of **Frame Trooper** come free on
  every deployment. Without it, 4 do.
- **Salvage insurance** keeps that share of a wiped haul's shards and parts.
  Blueprints and chassis in the haul are still lost.
- **Long-range scanner**: without it, the team sees what the tiles next to it
  hold. With it, it sees further.

## What the dock keeps

The **Stockpile** (the Loot page) holds your shards and components. The dock
also keeps, and the Stockpile lists under **Also kept here**:

- The Consumables and Prototypes you own.
- Pre-built copies waiting for the next deployment.

## Packing

On the World Map's **Deploy** screen, after choosing a Strike Team, **Pack**
lists the Consumables and Prototypes you own. Tap **+** and **−** to choose
how many of each to take.

- **Before each fight**, the setup offers only what's packed. Every packed
  Prototype comes along, and you choose which Consumables to bring, as in a
  free fight.
- **A Consumable used is gone.**
- **Home**: anything unused, and every Prototype, goes back to the dock.
- **A wipe** loses everything packed.

## Dev Mode

- **The Workshop's Pre-built tab** makes the pre-built Troops. They can use
  any chassis and any keyword, found or not, and they're never in your
  Designs, the Compendium or the Deck Builder. **↑** moves one earlier in the
  order the Exchange offers them.
- **What the Exchange carries**: tick the Consumables and Prototypes it
  sells, and set each price.
- **Prices and stock**, **Bounties** and **Upgrades**: every number on this
  page.
- **Restock now**, **Reach +1** and **Reset the dock**, for testing.

The dock's tables, stock and what it keeps for you are kept on this device.

## In the game

Game 0.15's dock is the Hangar, kept by **QM-7**, a Vantage Robotics logistics
unit: its portrait stands over the tabs, saying one line at a time about the
tab you opened, a team just home or lost, or something bought or refused. What
each tab is for is behind its **?**. The Exchange's goods are tiles, each with
its picture, a gold price tag and its buttons, and an empty section is a single
quiet line. QM-7's portrait and its blink are slots under **Game look** in the
Pixel Studio's Assets tab, like the rest of the game's look.
