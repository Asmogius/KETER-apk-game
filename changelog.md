## Revision 16.5 (Companion 14.26, game 0.42)

One rule from the campaign lab, which found Elites and Mini-Bosses turning Skull 3 POIs into walls once three Districts were cleared.

- **No Dimensional Instability below Skull 4.** Instability reaches only fights at Skull Level 4 and above (the POI's or the ambush's). A fight at Skull 1 to 3 has none, however many Districts are cleared. From Skull 4 it is as before: 3% for every District cleared, 15% at most.

## Revision 16.4 (Companion 14.24, game 0.40)

Three rules from the balance lab, which found long fights running out of cards and Units standing idle.

- **The Deck reshuffles.** When a card is to be drawn and the Deck is empty, the discard pile, all but its Troop cards, is shuffled to form a new Deck. A fallen Troop stays fallen. With nothing to shuffle, the draw is skipped as before. *Draw X* does the same.
- **A target in reach instead.** Auto and the Enemies choose a target, then move toward it. If it is still out of reach, they now attack another Unit in reach, chosen by the same Behavior keywords, rather than do nothing.
- **No wasted Actions.** Auto and the Enemies don't use an Action that deals no damage, heals nothing, does nothing else, and only gives the target a status it already has (or has only just shrugged off). They move to bring another Action in reach instead.

## Revision 16.3, clarified (Companion 14.12, game 0.23)

No new keywords; three things 16.3 left unsaid.

- **An Action can be aimed at a tile.** A Unit's Action that says *target tile* is aimed at any tile within its range (1 if it has none) that the Unit can see, its own included, and what it says is carried out there with no attack roll, as a card played from the hand is. Test Bulwark's Raise Wall puts down its Barricade this way. Auto never aims one.
- **Hardened cuts every hit**, a Poisoned or Bleed tick included, as each is a hit (never below 1).
- **Auto keeps a Line or a Cone off its own side**, as it already did an AoE.

## Revision 16.3

Eleven keywords, from the effect box audit: what the cards already said, that no keyword could write.

- **Troop** and **Hero**, narrowing words. Every Troop is a mechanical robot (Revision 15.5), so a card that names Mechanical Units on your side now says *Troop*.
- **all allies** and **all enemies**, Targets with no distance: a whole side, wherever it stands. They count like an area of 4 tiles (×3) for power.
- **Aim**, a Stat: ranged attacks need that much less to hit. The mirror of Evasive.
- **Hardened X**, a Status: each hit deals X less, never below 1.
- **Draw X** and **gain Aether X**, Effects for your side: cards from the Deck, Aether of the card's District (Neutral if it has none). A Chip whose Passive gains Aether gives that much more.
- **Barricade**, an Effect: a Half or Full Cover Barricade on an empty target tile, for the rest of the fight.
- **Line X** and **Cone X**, Modifiers beside AoE: the attack also hits every Unit in a straight run of X tiles through its target, or in a wedge X tiles deep toward it, friend or foe.
- **SPOOL stays retired.** The cards that still named it are rewritten with Stats that build up over the rounds: Rictus's Spin-Up gains +2 DMG and +1 MS each round for 3 turns, so he reaches +6 and +3 by his third turn.
- **Every new keyword is a blueprint** in the Workshop: the numbered ones in three levels.

## Revision 16.2

Two small rules, for content batch 1 (District 3, fast melee).

- **A Support Action aimed only at *this Unit* needs no target.** It is used on the Unit itself, at once, for its Energy, like a stance or a burst of speed (*this Unit gains Dash 2*).
- **Cards in the haul.** An Elite (1 in 4), a Mini-Boss (1 in 2), a Boss (always), a Cache (1 in 5) and a POI's reward (3 in 10) can drop a Consumable or a Prototype card: one of that District's or of no District, no rarer than the Skull Level allows (Skull 1 Common, Skull 2 up to Uncommon, and so on). It rides home in the haul and is kept at the dock. A card an Enemy names in its own drops (*Grav-Boot Charge, 25%*) drops the same way.

## Revision 16.1

Four movement keywords, for Units that fight on the move.

- **Lunge X**, a Modifier on a Melee Action. Before attacking, the Unit may move up to X tiles to reach its target, so it can strike something X + 1 tiles away. The move is free (no Movement, no Energy), walks round walls and Units, and ends beside the target: off a hazard where it can, then the shortest walk, then the tile beside the most Cover. Its tiles count toward Charge.
- **Charge X**, a Modifier on a Melee Action. +X damage if the Unit has moved 3 or more tiles this turn before the attack lands: walking, a Lunge, a Leap and a Dash count, a Push does not.
- **Dash X**, an Effect. One of yours gains X extra tiles of Movement this turn, to use before or after acting. It is not a Stat, so Slowed does not cut it; Rooted and Stunned still stop it. An Enemy uses its Dash at once, moving away from the Unit it attacked: hit and run.
- **Leap X**, an Effect, like Flying. The Unit can jump to any empty tile within X that its Movement can pay for, over Units, Cover, walls, gaps and hazards; only where it lands counts. With a Duration it lasts that long; without one, for the rest of the fight.
- **Power.** Lunge and Charge add to an attack's worth (1 a tile, 1 a point of damage) rather than multiplying it; Dash and Leap are worth 1 a tile.

## Revision 16.0

A playtest found abilities that locked an Enemy down for a whole fight, killed anything outright, or cost nothing. These rules close them.

- **The locks.** Stunned, Rooted, Pinned and Marked last 2 turns at most, however long a card gives them for. A second dose does nothing while the first lasts, so a Burst gives one lock, not three. When a lock ends, the Unit is guarded: it cannot be given the same lock again until the same point of the next round. An always-on Passive cannot give a lock; a Passive that reacts with *when* can.
- **What an attack carries needs it to Penetrate.** The Statuses, Pushes, extra damage and other effects written on a Melee, Ranged or Cyber attack land only when it gets through Armor Class, or Integrity for a Cyber attack. Support Actions and Aether Damage always land.
- **Execute** joins the Effects. It removes an Enemy below half its Health outright. An Elite, a Mini-Boss or a Boss never is, nor an Enemy at half its Health or more.
- **Sacrifice never touches an Enemy**, and nothing is refunded for it.
- **An area costs more.** Where power is worked out from keywords, a sentence aimed at *all enemies*, *all allies* or *all units within* N tiles counts (1 + 0.5 × N) times what it carries.
- **Overclock has no rules in a fight yet.** It does nothing and costs nothing until it is written.


## Revision 15.9

One rule changed: when a Status ticks.

- **A Status ticks at the end of the turn after the one it arrived in.** Every Status used to tick in the Recharge Phase. One given in the Enemies' turn therefore lost a turn before the Player had taken one: a Hero Stunned for a turn was never Stunned in a turn of their own, and Poison for two turns from an Enemy did its second and last damage before the Player could answer it. Now the round has two halves. A Status that arrives in the Player's half (from the Recharge Phase to the end of the Player's turn) ticks at the next Recharge Phase, as before. One that arrives in the Enemies' half (their turn, and the Waves, Aether and Draw phases after it) ticks as the Player's next turn ends, before the Enemies act.
- **What it means at the table.** A Status of 1 turn lasts through the whole of the next turn, whoever gave it. A second dose keeps the timing of the first. A Stats keyword with a Duration in turns counts down the same way.
- **Nothing else moves.** Statuses given in the Player's turn, and a Unit's own boosts in its own turn, last exactly as long as they did.

## Revision 15.8

One rule removed, one finished, one made exact.

- **Usability Restrictions are removed.** An Equipment, Action or Command Card could name a subtype or Archetypes that alone might use it. No longer: any Hero may be assigned any Action Card, any Unit may use any Equipment, and any Troop may perform any Command. Those three kinds of card no longer carry Archetypes, which are labels for targeting Units and nothing else.
- **Variable Costs, in full.** X is at least 1 and at most what the Bank can pay; XX is the same X paid twice. X may stand wherever a keyword takes a number (*damage X*, *+X HP*, *for X turns*, *Deploy X Aether Battery*), and a stat may be written N(X), N for each X. A variable-cost Troop that arrives unpaid (a Starting Troop, or one put down by a Deploy) has X = 1. An Action Card with a variable Aether cost asks for X at each use. For the Aether Pouch's minimum, X counts as 1.
- **Dimensional Instability with nothing to send.** The Elite or Mini-Boss is chosen from those of the current District. If the District has none of the kind rolled, the Enemy that was drawn arrives after all.


## Revision 15.7

One keyword, for an effect the rules already had and no card could say.

- **Recover** joins the Effects. It returns a Troop's card from the Discard Pile to your hand, chosen by whoever plays it; the Troop is played again at its normal cost. This is what a Repair Kit does. With no Troop card in the Discard Pile, a card that Recovers cannot be played.
- **When a card reaches the Discard Pile**, said in one place. A Troop's card stays with its Troop until the Troop is Incapacitated. An Equipment Card stays with the Unit wearing it until it is removed or that Unit is Incapacitated. Commands and Aether Cards are discarded as they are played. Nothing here is new; it is what Sections 3 and 6 already said, and what Recover relies on.


## Revision 15.3

Three questions the rules had not answered, settled before the Simulator carries them out.

- **Line of sight is tile to tile.** A Unit sees a target if any point in its tile can be joined to any point in the target's tile by a straight line touching no wall. A line touching a wall at all does not count, so two walls meeting at a corner seal it.
- **Target ally means another friendly Unit.** An effect on its own Unit says *this Unit*.
- **A Push blocked by a Unit hurts both.** The pushed Unit and the one it hit each take 1 damage for every tile the push could not complete.


## Revision 15.2

Three gaps found while working out how the Simulator would carry the keywords out.

- **"When Incapacitated"** joins the Conditions. Sacrifice already said that anything triggering on a Unit going down still triggers, but nothing could express such a trigger.
- **"While attached"** joins the Durations: an effect lasts as long as the card granting it stays on the Unit, so a bonus from Equipment ends when that Equipment is removed.
- **"remove"** joins the Effects, naming what it takes off — a piece of Equipment or a Status. Nothing could previously lift a Poisoned or strip a piece of gear.
- **A Modify always carries a Duration**, because a stat change that resolves at once changes nothing.

Settled at the same time, from questions the rules had never answered:

- **Statuses apply outright** — there is no roll to resist one — and a repeat **adds** its number to what is already there.
- **Statuses tick in the Recharge Phase**, for everyone at once. Bleed's 10% rounds to the nearest whole number, never less than 1.
- An **automatic Overwhelmed** lasts until the end of the next Recharge, one full round.
- **Suppressive Fire** may only be held by a Unit whose card grants it, and only if it has not moved. The Enemy shot carries on moving, and an unused hold lapses with the Enemy turn.


## Revision 15.1

A correction to Revision 15.0, before any of it was built.

- **Amount is withdrawn as a category.** Five colours remain: Condition violet, Target cyan, Effect amber, Status crimson, Duration pink. A number travels with its keyword and takes its colour, so there are no loose numbers to colour.
- **Modify** is added as an Effect keyword, naming the stat and the amount: *Modify AC +2*, *Modify DMG −1*. It replaces what the Amount category was there to carry.
- Effect text therefore reads **Condition, Target, Effect, Duration**.


## Revision 15.0

Effect text becomes something the rules can read, and the Battle Simulator can carry out.

### Effect text

- **Rules words on a card are coloured**, and white text is flavour the rules ignore. Six colours, each a category: **Condition** violet, **Target** cyan, **Effect** amber, **Status** crimson, **Amount** green, **Duration** pink.
- **One sentence is one effect**, read as Condition → Target → Effect → Amount → Duration, so it is never ambiguous which number belongs to which effect.
- A number travels with its keyword: *Rooted 3* is one thing.
- **Duration** is optional: 2 to 10 turns, or persistent. Without one, an effect resolves at once.
- Only keywords from the library may be coloured, so anything marked is something the rules can carry out.
- The `[[bracket]]` notation is withdrawn. Colour marks a keyword now.

### The keyword library

- **Ten Statuses**: Rooted, Stunned, Slowed, Marked, Pinned, Poisoned, Bleed, Weakened, Sundered, Drained — each saying what its own number means, because they differ.
- **Ten Effects**: Damage, Heal, Push, Pierce, Chain, AoE, Deploy, Sacrifice, Suppressive Fire, Flying.
- **Four Conditions**: if damaged, when deployed, if this Unit kills an Enemy, and distance comparisons.
- **Marked** now means ranged attacks always hit, and **Pinned** that Suppressive Fire always hits.
- **Sacrifice** counts as Incapacitated, so effects that trigger on a Unit going down still trigger.
- **Deploy** places a Unit on a friendly Spawn Tile unless the card says *adjacent*.

### New rules

- **Line of sight.** Only walls block it. Cover makes a target harder to hit without hiding it, and Units never block a shot.
- **Targeting.** A Target may be a Unit or a tile, and may name more than one subtype.
- **Reactions.** A Unit may hold fire with Suppressive Fire, paying the Energy when it holds, and shoots the first Enemy into range during the Enemy turn.

### Withdrawn

- **SPOOL** and **Splash**.
- Three conditions other wording already covered: *if this penetrates*, *when combat begins*, and triggers waiting on a Status being gained or an Enemy attacking.


## Revision 14.0

A structural revision. Strike Teams, Decks, Leadership, Aether Chips and how Enemies arrive have all changed, and the Glossary has been brought into line with every one of them.

### Strike Teams and Heroes

- **A Strike Team holds up to 3 Heroes, one shared Deck and one Aether Pouch.** A deployment brings one Strike Team.
- **Floor size now decides how many Heroes fight** — 1 on a Small Floor, 2 on Medium, 3 on Large — where it used to decide how many Strike Teams deployed.
- **One Hero leads each Combat** and always enters it. Leading has no other effect.
- **A Combat is lost only when every Hero present is down.** A downed Hero leaves with their Action Cards; the Deck, hand and Troops stay. Heroes held back by a smaller Floor are the reserves that allow a retry.

### The Deck

- **One Deck of 10 to 60 cards,** shared by every Hero. 6 cards open the Combat, then 1 per Hero standing each Draw Phase.
- **Action Cards are no longer part of the Deck.** They are assigned to Heroes' Action Slots before a Combat, kept between Combats, and changeable before any of them. Each prints its use cost: Energy, Aether, or both.
- **Combat Role no longer restricts anything.** Any Unit may use any card; Combat Role describes how a Unit fights.
- **Affinity no longer restricts who may use a card.** The Aether Chip is the only gate: a card of any District may go in the Deck, but it can only be paid for with that District's Aether.

### Leadership

- **Leadership now sets the Starting Troops** — Troops taken out of the Deck before a Combat and placed on Player Spawn Tiles at no cost. Several Heroes pool their Leadership.
- **Troops played during Combat are no longer capped,** but each needs an open Player Spawn Tile.

### Aether

- **Drawn Chips stay in the Aether Bank for the rest of the Combat,** recharging each turn, so the Bank grows as the fight goes on. Unspent Aether is still lost at the end of each turn.
- **The Aether Pouch is chosen before each Combat.** It must hold at least as many Chips as the full cost of the most expensive card in the Deck; there is no maximum.
- **Six Basic Aether Chips,** one per District, worth 1 Aether each, are always available.
- **Every Chip prints its own Aether value, 0 to 10.** Level and Rarity are now the guideline for choosing it rather than the rule. A Chip with an effect fires it when spent.
- Fixed: the chip rules disagreed with themselves — one passage drew a Chip per Hero, another per Player Level. It is Player Level.

### Enemies

- **Floors are fought in 6 waves.** Each wave fills every Enemy Spawn Tile; the next arrives when the wave is cleared or a Wave Timer, set before the Combat, runs out.
- **Every Enemy Spawn Tile carries its own Skull Level,** and draws an Enemy of that Level by the Reveal Rule. When the Enemy Card Pile runs out, no more Enemies arrive.
- **Elites** are a new Enemy tier and card type, between ordinary Enemies and Mini-Bosses.
- **Dimensional Instability:** each District cleared adds a 3% chance, up to 15%, that a spawn produces an Elite or a Mini-Boss instead. Mini-Bosses are no longer mixed into the Enemy Card Piles.

## Revision 13.6

Every revision of the Player Handbook, newest first. The Handbook itself no
longer carries this — it lives here so the rules text stays rules text.

This revision reconciles the Handbook with the KETER Card Forge, which had moved ahead of it. It adds a new Card Reference section covering the twelve card types, stat ranges, the corner cluster, and the Level scaling system, and settles two terminology conflicts.


### Reconciled with the Card Forge

- **Aetherite Sphere renamed Aether Chip. **The Aether-generating token is now the Aether Chip, a 39mm circular card type. Aether Sphere Draw becomes Aether Draw, and the Sphere Bank becomes the Aether Bank. The Aether Pouch keeps its name. Note this reuses a name previously retired as a currency — the old Aether Chips currency remains retired, and Aether Shards are still the sole currency.

- **Unit Card renamed Troop Card. **“Unit” now refers only to Heroes and Troops collectively; the card that deploys a Troop is a Troop Card.

- **Combat Role icons defined. **Melee is a fist, Ranged a crosshair, Support a cross, with the Mechanical Archetype printing alongside as a gear. This closes the open item from v11 and avoids the collision with the Action Slots sword glyph.

- **New Section 12, Card Reference. **Documents the twelve card types with their stat lines and effect caps, stat ranges, standing reminder lines, the corner cluster, Rarity, user restrictions, Level scaling, and card numbering. The Glossary moves to Section 13.

- **Intelligence is a printed Enemy stat. **INT 1–6 now appears in the Enemy stat table, having previously been described in prose only.

- **Mini-Boss and Boss are distinct card types. **Both use the Enemy stat line but are not bound by Enemy ranges.

- **Card Levels and scaling documented. **Cards carry a Level 1–6. HP and Action Damage scale ×1.00 to ×2.75 across the six levels; everything else is frozen. Scaling is baked onto the card and recomputed from a Level 1 base so it never compounds.

- **Rarity sets Aether generation. **An Aether Chip generates Aether equal to its Rarity tier, Common 1 through Mythic 6. Rarity is shown by the colour of the card name rather than a printed word.

- **Affinity Levels capped at 6. **Hero Affinity pips total no more than 6 across both Affinities, matching the Player Level ceiling.

- **Effects carry their own Combat Role. **A single card may hold a Melee effect and a Ranged effect side by side.

### Carried Forward from Version 11.0

- Every Unit and every Action, Command, and Equipment Card carries a Combat Role; one shared Role is enough to equip.

- Aether and Consumable Cards carry no Combat Role and may be used by any Unit.

- Combat Role is separate from Archetype and may be referenced by card effects for targeting.

### How Attrition Now Works

Nothing is lost permanently through combat damage. Pressure comes from three places instead:

- **Hero availability. **Each Hero lost is gone for the rest of the deployment, shrinking both your fielded strength and your reserve pool as a run continues.

- **Committed resources. **Consumables are locked in per POI and Decks are locked for the whole deployment, so poor selection cannot be corrected in the field.

- **Banked loot at risk. **Everything collected sits unusable in the Deployment Inventory until you return. The longer a run goes, the more a wipe costs.

Attempting the largest Floor your Level permits leaves no reserves and therefore no retry. Choosing a smaller Floor is what buys a safety net.

### Carried Forward from Earlier Revisions

- Deployment size equals Player Level; Floor sizes gated L1 Small, L2 Small/Medium, L3+ any.

- Reserve Heroes allow free unlimited Combat retries; a Deployment Wipe occurs only when no reserve remains.

- Decks are access-locked for the whole deployment; Consumables are committed per POI.

- An Incapacitated Hero is out for the rest of the deployment and does not count as a reserve.

- Medical Supplies and Repair Kits are in-Combat Consumables returning a Troop's card to hand at full Aether cost.

- The Mechanical Archetype determines which recovery Consumable applies.

- All discarded Cards return to their Deck at the end of every Combat.

- Losing a Hero removes their Deck and all Troops deployed from it for the rest of that Combat.

- Starting AP removed; Heroes begin combat with 0 AP unless the card states otherwise.

- Damage is not a Unit stat; it is carried by individual Actions.

- Armor renamed Armor Class (AC); Health renamed Health Points (HP).

- The former Aether Chips currency was retired; Aether Shards are the sole currency. The name Aether Chip is now reused for the Aether-generating token, formerly the Aetherite Sphere.

- Drained X is half Movement Speed only.

- AP and Aether are separate resources: AP carries over, Aether is lost at end of turn.

- Squad retired; Battlefield capacity is measured in Strike Teams.

- Combat Card subtypes are Unit, Equipment, Command, and Aether.

- Roster Deck and Recruitment Roster retired; Hero and Troop Cards live in the general Loot pool, obtained via the Reveal Rule.

- POI difficulty always matches Player Level, with exceptions stated on the POI Card.

- All Keycards are Prototypes; some are consumed on use.

- Captain retired; each Strike Team contains exactly 1 Hero.

- Cyber Damage retired; Cyborg keeps its Status Effect vulnerability.

- Range 20 auto-Penetration retired; Overrun Hideout loot matches Player Level.

- Investigation Cards are drawn when the Player moves without landing on an unexplored tile.

- Unit subtypes folded into the Archetype lists.

- Draw Phase is skipped for any Deck with no cards remaining.

- Battlefield size caps only the number of Strike Teams deployed.
