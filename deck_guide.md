# **Deck Builder Guide**

The Deck Builder is where a Loadout is assembled: the one Strike Team that
leaves the Hangar, with its Heroes, their shared Deck, the Action Cards each
Hero carries, and the Aether Pouch.

## Loadout, Strike Team, Deck

Three words that are easy to confuse, so plainly:

A **Loadout** *is* a Strike Team. One leaves the Hangar on a deployment.

A **Strike Team** is up to **3 Heroes**. The Floor decides how many actually
enter a Combat — 1 on a Small Floor, 2 on Medium, 3 on Large — and you choose
one of them to **lead** each Combat. Heroes left out are your reserves: they are
what lets a lost Combat be retried, so bringing more Heroes than a Floor admits
is what buys a safety net.

A **Deck** is **one** Deck of 10 to 60 cards, shared by every Hero in the team.
You draw 6 at the start of a Combat, then 1 per Hero standing each Draw Phase.

**On the World Map** (the video game), a Strike Team's Troops must be designs
from the Workshop, and each copy in its Deck is paid for in parts when it
deploys. While it's out, it's marked **Out on a deployment** here and opens
locked until it comes home.

## Building one

**New** creates a Loadout. Name it and set the Player Level, which is how many
Aether Chips you draw each turn.

**Add Hero** adds one, up to three. Tap a Hero in the list to open what they
carry.

**Deck** opens the shared Deck. The picker leaves out anything that cannot be in
one: Heroes, Enemies, Elites, Prototypes, Consumables, Aether Chips — and
**Action Cards**, which are no longer part of a Deck at all.

**Action Cards** are assigned to a Hero, up to that Hero's Action Slots, and are
carried into Combat by them. Empty slots are fine, and you can change the
assignment before any Combat. Each card prints what a use costs: Energy, Aether,
or both.

**Aether Pouch** is what you draw Chips from during a Combat. Six **Basic
Chips**, one per District, are always available and worth 1 Aether each; your own
Chips sit beneath them. Drawn Chips stay in the Bank for the rest of the fight,
so a fuller Pouch means the Bank keeps growing for longer.

## What it checks, and why it never blocks you

Every check pings with the Handbook section behind it, and none of them stop
you. The Deck Builder tells you what the rules say and lets you decide.

The checks worth knowing:

**Deck size.** Under 10 or over 60.

**Action Cards in the Deck.** They cannot be there; assign them to a Hero.

**Action Slots.** A Hero carrying more Action Cards than they have slots.

**The Pouch minimum.** It must hold at least as many Chips as the full cost —
generic plus District — of the most expensive card in the Deck. A Deck whose
priciest card costs 6 needs at least 6 Chips. That counts Chips, not what they
are worth.

**District matching.** Cards that need a District's Aether when the Pouch holds
no Chip of that District. They are legal, but nothing can pay for them.

Combat Role is no longer checked. Under Revision 14.0 any Unit may use any card,
and the matching Aether Chip is the only gate.

A Loadout showing problems still loads in the Simulator. It will simply behave
the way the rules say it behaves.

## Editing

A Loadout's name and Player Level are editable in place. **Delete** asks before
it removes anything; the cards themselves are untouched.

**Import loadout** takes a Loadout exported from another device, and each
Loadout can be exported the same way.

## Loadouts made before Revision 14.0

They are converted the first time you open the Deck Builder, and it tells you
what it did. A Loadout that held several Strike Teams becomes one Loadout per
Hero, so nothing is merged. Action Cards are taken out of Decks, since they can
no longer be there — the cards stay in your collection, ready to assign, and
every Hero's slots start empty for you to fill.
